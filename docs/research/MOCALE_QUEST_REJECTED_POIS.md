# Mocale Quest — Rejected / Deferred POIs

**Research run:** 2026-08-18

These entries are intentionally retained so later agents do not blindly rediscover them.

| Place | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Former Parco Naturale di Cavriglia animal park | **Reject as animal attraction** | The old animal-park concept is stale. Current historical information indicates the animals were removed after the structure failed; it must not appear as a live zoo/farm quest. |
| Parco Zoo di Poppi | **Defer / likely reject** | Family-oriented on paper, but recent reviews describe dated infrastructure and difficulty seeing animals. Too much drive for uncertain payoff. |
| l'altroieri, Montevarchi | **Defer** | Excellent current rating, but €50–90 positioning makes it poor fit for spontaneous, low-friction MVP food loops. |
| Adventure Park Il Gigante | **Defer** | Strong reviews and currently open, but toward Florence/Pratolino and likely outside the desired lightweight radius. Age/height rules need a dedicated check. |
| Adrenaline Adventure Park Casentino | **Defer** | Interesting and currently operating, but long enough drive to compete with better local quests. Age/height constraints need verification. |
| Full Acqua Zolfina 7 km loop with small children in heat | **Do not recommend automatically** | Keep the Balze and trail in the dataset, but the full loop should be suppressed in extreme heat/high fatigue. |
| Lago di San Cipriano as a swimming location | **Reject claim** | Official tourism material supports relaxation, fishing, sailing/canoeing; it does not establish public swimming suitability. Do not infer swimming. |
| Generic Florence day trip | **Defer from core 30–40** | Great destination, wrong product shape for the local spontaneous loop. Add later as an exceptional long quest. |
| Siena / San Gimignano / Val d'Orcia checklist trips | **Reject from MVP radius** | Famous but too much driving for this base and the game's low-friction, dynamic-next-action philosophy. |
